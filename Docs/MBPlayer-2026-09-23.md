# MBPlayer reinspection · September 23, 2026

Scope: current anonymous Taiwan/Chinese web experience and developer-supplied Android/iOS
store materials. No access to MixerBox's internal recommendation model, training data,
ranking scores or logged-in native app experiments. App store screenshots are promotional
references, not proof that every current device receives the same layout.

## Observed source contract

- https://www.mbplayer.com/ (web version 1.0.88-11) shows song-seeded recommendation mixes,
  charts, genre/mood editorial shelves, featured playlists and new releases; podcasts are
  separate. Home contains actual music cards, not merely category navigation.
- Public web code calls `POST /api/getPage?page=Home` with history when available. Our service
  always sends `{}` and never imports browser/player history. The observed response contains
  two `recommendPlaylists` shelves, each with ten `RD + videoID` mixes and `autoPlay: true`.
  That is evidence of a song-led entry point; it does not reveal the proprietary ranking model.
- `POST /api/page/getSearch?page=Search` returns a root `items` array. Its twelve displayed
  genre choices rotate between requests. It also returns complete music carousels (charts,
  commute, chill, hip-hop, party, good mood), whose `vectorId` attributes were ignored by v2.
  Genre discovery must accumulate; overwriting the registry loses categories.
- `GET /api/getVector?vectorId=...&skip=...&limit=...` provides genre cards. The inspected
  C-pop page reports 22 total items, including useful lists beyond the old 12-item head.
- `GET /api/playlist?reverse=true&type=playlist&vectorId=...` returns the full ordered list
  in the inspected examples, with `next: null`. The native website uses the same reverse
  parameter. `itemCount` counts returned entries; `total` can include unavailable tracks.
  Inline preview entries are not full lists.
- The C-pop card “熱門華語主打” opens a detailed list named “Mando Flow”, with 69 usable songs
  and total 71. Keep the localized discovery title; source detail titles may differ.
- ID 193429561 “本月熱門精選” has 11 returned songs, total 13, created August 2023. Its presence
  in a “本週” shelf does not make it a new release. `generatedAt` must never imply music age.
- A song-seeded `RDnkflJ5P0g6c` mix returned 25 songs, total 26. The seed was first. That first
  song makes the choice concrete and is suitable for a truthful card preview.

The old CDN had 60 lists at 2026-09-23 04:50 UTC, with no genre:cpop/kpop/jpop/pop labels.
Its rotating genre discovery plus positional hydration cursor underrepresented those areas.
The first bounded v3 probe made two page reads, two vector reads and eighteen playlist reads;
17 lists validated and one incomplete list was rejected. It produced 68 complete lists,
4,109 list-track occurrences and 3,839 unique video IDs. This does not claim 3,839 successful
streaming tests or new unique recordings (different videos may represent the same song).

## Product lessons

The Android store highlights automatic lists based on favorite artists, national charts,
continuous playback and library synchronization. Official screenshots show play controls
on recognizable chart rows and grouped genre/mood cards.
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=zh_TW&id=mbinc12.mb32
https://tw.mixerbox.com/oneplayer

The iOS destination now emphasizes MixerBox AI and voice content, so it must not be treated
as identical to Android MB3. Individual reviews and developer replies mention recent-playlist
retrieval and similar-song discovery; these are qualitative signals, not population estimates.
https://apps.apple.com/tw/app/id1584951634

**Inference for Music 日常:** give listeners something recognizable to start with, reduce the
number of decisions before playback, then continue a coherent queue. A larger random catalog
alone cannot achieve that. Our feed stays a simple two-column stream; previews use real songs,
not invented artist names or generic “for you” claims. Stable scrolling and buffered switching
remain part of the conversion path.

## Measurement contract

Primary: the fraction of measurable home-feed sessions with a deliberate selection followed
by a real playback-start event. The client defines start as at least 0.5 seconds of advancing
playback, not URL resolution or a button tap. It cannot establish speaker audibility remotely.
Count a session once; automatic queue continuation after mere exposure is not a new conversion.
Inspect cold/learning/established users separately.

Guardrails: actual 30-second listening, early skip, failure rate, click-to-start latency,
repeat exposure and return listening. A start is valuable only when it is technically successful
and gives a useful listening experience. No claimed lift without a baseline and a controlled
comparison. Source tags and preview affinities are an explainable initial strategy, not a
trained probability model or a reproduction of MixerBox's algorithm.

The three earlier successful detail probes were reused with their original observation times
instead of fetched again. The migration checkpoint therefore contains 71 complete lists,
4,214 list-track occurrences and 3,926 distinct video IDs, including the tested monthly
featured list and current C-pop mix. The catalog is approximately 78 KB including previews.
